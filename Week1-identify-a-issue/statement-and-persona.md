# AI 视频协作与资产管理工具

## Project Statement

Professional AI filmmaking often requires multiple creators to collaborate on the same project. However, shot assignments, source materials, prompts, generative models, and different versions are often stored on individual devices or across separate platforms such as Kling AI and Seedance. This makes it difficult to hand off assets and prompts, resulting in considerable redundant work, such as repeatedly transferring assets and prompts between team members.

This project aims to design a collaborative platform for small AI filmmaking teams. Team members will be able to divide responsibilities by shot, share complete generation histories, manage creative assets, and seamlessly take over one another's work. The goal is to create a more connected and traceable production workflow while exploring a viable business model for professional teams.

Similar to Claude Code's software, the platform will allow users to choose different large language models and video generation models for each task.
